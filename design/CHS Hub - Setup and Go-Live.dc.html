Setting up the Program Hub

Everything that has to happen once, before the first coach signs in. Work through it in order; nothing here needs to be done twice.

The hub arrives loaded with practice content — players called "Player 1", opponents called "Opponent 3", a made-up schedule. That content is there so every page has something to show. Setup is the job of clearing it out and putting the real program in its place.

Two kinds of work are involved. Most of it you do by clicking around inside the hub, and it is covered step by step below. A short list of jobs can only be done by editing the hub file itself; those are gathered in Part 3 so you can hand them over in one go.

Read this first

The hub saves its information in the web browser on the computer where it is used. There is no shared server behind it. A record typed on the coaching office computer does not appear on a laptop at home, and clearing that browser's history or site data erases everything the hub has stored.

Pick one computer and one browser as the program's record — the machine where schedules, results and rosters get typed. Everyone else can open the hub to look at their own copy, but that one machine holds the season.

Part 1 — Clear the practice data

Do this before anything else, on the computer you chose above. Everything the hub has remembered — the sample schedule, roster, results and announcements — is wiped and the hub starts from a clean slate.

  1. Open the hub in the browser you have chosen.
  2. Open the browser's settings and find the option to clear browsing data or site data.
  3. Clear cookies and site data for the hub's address only, not your whole history.
  4. Reload the hub. It comes back with the sample content again — that is expected, because the samples are built into the file. Part 3 covers removing them for good.

If you would rather not touch browser settings, skip this step and hand it over with the Part 3 list instead.

Part 2 — Build the program, in this order

Order matters. Teams have to exist before you can put a player on one, and staff have to exist before anyone can sign in as a coach. Every one of these steps is done inside the hub, and the coach guide has the click-by-click detail for each.

  1. Teams. Manage → Teams. Varsity is permanent. Add each other squad exactly as you want it named everywhere — every table, filter and dropdown in the hub is built from this list.
  2. Opponents. Same page, lower down. Add every school you expect to play, spelled consistently. All-time head-to-head records are matched on these names, so "St. Paul's" and "St Pauls" become two different opponents forever.
  3. Staff. Manage → Staff. Add every coach, plus the principal and athletic director if you want them printed on the game day roster sheet. The hub assigns each person a four-digit PIN automatically and shows it on their card.
  4. Season. History → Seasons. Confirm the label for the season you are about to play, and that it is the only one marked current.
  5. Roster. This Season → Roster. Add every player with jersey number, team, grade and a four-digit PIN of your choosing. Tick the captain box for captains — the hub records them as an honor when the season closes.
  6. Schedule. This Season → Schedule. Enter every practice, game and scrimmage you know about. Tick district game on the district fixtures as you go; it is easy to forget and it drives the district record.
  7. Team goals (optional). This Season → Team Goals. Varsity targets for the year. The hub fills in the actual figures as the season runs.

Part 3 — Jobs that need the file edited

These cannot be done by clicking. Hand this list, as it stands, to whoever maintains the hub file.

Hand-over list
Turn off the demo date
The hub currently pretends the date is 20 August 2026 so the sample pages have something to show. Set DEMO_TODAY_ISO to empty and it uses the real calendar.
Remove the built-in sample content
The starting schedule, roster, staff, announcements, game reports, team stats and previous season are written into the file. Until they are emptied, they come back whenever the hub is opened on a fresh computer.
Drop in the two images
The school crest in the sidebar, and the "C" logo that prints at the top of the game day roster sheet.
Real names for the principal and athletic director
Alternatively, add them as staff with the role Principal or Athletic Director and the hub will use those instead. That is the easier route and needs no file editing.

None of these stop you using the hub. Until the first two are done, though, a fresh computer will always show sample content, and dates will read as August 2026.

Part 4 — Handing out PINs

Everyone signs in with a four-digit PIN, and the PIN decides what they see. A coach PIN unlocks everything. A player PIN opens a read-only view of that player's own team, starting on their availability check-in.

  • Coach PINs are on each staff card, under Manage → Staff.
  • Player PINs are on each player card, under This Season → Roster, and are yours to set.
  • No two people can share a PIN. The hub will not let you save a duplicate.

Send players their PIN with the hub's address and one line on what to do with it: sign in, check the schedule, answer yes or no to each event.

Part 5 — Test run before you announce it

Twenty minutes, on the record computer, before anyone else gets the address.

  1. Sign in with your own coach PIN. The overview should name the next real event on your schedule.
  2. Sign in as a player using one player's PIN, on a phone if you can. Check the schedule looks right and the yes/no buttons work.
  3. Enter a fake result on a past friendly, look at Stats to confirm the numbers moved, then remove it again.
  4. Post an announcement and check it appears on the home page.
  5. Print a game day roster sheet for your first fixture.

If all five work, the hub is ready. Hand the coach guide to your staff.

Keeping a copy

Because the season lives in one browser on one computer, it is worth keeping something on paper or in a file that does not depend on that machine.

  • Monthly: Manage → Reports → Export CSV, on both the attendance and season stats tabs. Save the files somewhere shared.
  • End of season: print or save the season stats report and the seasons page before closing the season.
  • Do not let anyone clear site data on the record computer without exporting first.

Closed seasons are locked once the season is closed, so past records cannot drift. That protects history inside the hub; it does not protect against the computer being wiped.
